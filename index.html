01

Built for institutions

Founded September 2023 · JP Nagar & Rajajinagar, Bengaluru

Athvie Global started with a focused mission: help institutions move faster through better technology and strategy. We’ve grown across two offices in Bengaluru — an expansion that reflects the demand we’re seeing. Institutions are ready for change, and they need partners who can deliver it.

Our approach integrates marketing and technology into systems that deliver measurable impact. We align our success with our clients' long-term outcomes — we take equity positions in ventures we’re invested in, which means we’re committed to sustained growth rather than short-term engagements.

Our process is AI-native throughout — product design, engineering, data analysis and strategy — with product, engineering, marketing and data operating as one cross-functional team. We move fast, experiment, and scale what works. Most institutions run on fragmented systems and outdated processes; we rebuild them to be more efficient, more integrated and more effective.

03

Many industries, one playbook

Every sector we’ve delivered in becomes part of the Athvie story.

01 Entertainment Films that became movements

Spearheaded marketing, communication and PR for major Indian film franchises — Salaar, Kantara, Mahavatar Narsimha, Yuva, Bagheera and Dhoomam — positioning each release as a cultural event through multi-platform strategy, digital-first execution, earned PR, and influencer & meme ecosystems.

Salaar Fan Army — one of Indian cinema’s most innovative fan-engagement models: AR filters, personalised Army IDs, tags and numbers. 1.2 lakh+ verified registrations.

Salaar Creative Squad — fans designed posters and artwork, blurring the line between consumers and creators.

Kantara: A Legend Chapter-1 Auditions — a tech-driven audition process managing 25,000+ applications with transparent, scalable digital shortlisting.

Kantara Talent Tribe — grassroots performances inspired by Kantara, connecting cultural pride to mainstream cinema.

Brands of Bharat — 200+ culturally rooted Indian brands applied; the top 10 were featured with Kantara through HombaleVerse, taken from local to global.

HombaleVerse — India’s first production-house merchandise platform: ₹1 crore+ revenue within 3 months of the Salaar campaign.

Also: Superman merchandise for PVR-INOX, and the website for Astra Studios — a premium VFX & creative studio.

02 Education Campuses taken digital

S-VYASA Deemed-to-be University, City Campus — strategic PR, influencer marketing and launch ideation delivering 1 crore+ impressions and 1,000+ admissions in the first year.

DigiVyasa — conceptualised and launched a digital platform taking structured Indian yoga education global (Yoga Instructor’s Course, Yoga for Diabetes, Yoga for Hypertension, Stress Management). Launched by Union Health Minister Shri J. P. Nadda.

IIM Bangalore — contributed course design for the BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship, structuring curriculum around interviews with CEOs of top companies.

University events — Empuraan anti-drug campaign at SVYASA with Karnataka State Police, Mohanlal and Prithviraj; Yuva at PES University; Bagheera at RV College of Engineering; Salaar at Global Academy of Technology.

03 Healthcare Care, communicated with dignity

NIMHANS — built the complaint & grievance management platform for the National Institute of Mental Health and Neurosciences: digital intake, routing, status tracking and auditable records in a single system.

Cadabams Group — designed and led PR, brand placement and storytelling across nine brands, making mental health, rehabilitation and wellness relatable to youth, families, corporates and seniors:
Cadabams Amitha · MindTalk · WeNest · Anunitha · Care by Cadabams · Physiotattva · Child Development Centre · Cadabams Hospitals · Diagnostic Centres.

04 Political Campaign infrastructure, built like a product

Rajthantra — a technology platform for campaign and constituency operations, running as three purpose-built tools: the Janasevaka citizen-engagement app, a Booth Management System for field operations at polling-booth granularity, and a telecalling platform for voter outreach at scale.

Managed social media, digital strategy and public reputation for the then Deputy Chief Minister of Karnataka, Dr. C. N. Ashwath Narayan — and for Dr. Ranganath (Kunigal) plus three MLA candidates in the 2023 Karnataka elections.

Narrative development for the Congress party in collaboration with Prof. Trilochan Sastry (IIM-B) and Dr. G. K. Jayaram — positioning messaging around governance, accountability and citizen-centric development.

05 Agriculture From farm to ward

Organic Mandya — collaborated with the leading social enterprise for organic farming. Developed the "One Village to One Ward" concept in Malleshwaram, Bengaluru: connecting rural farmers directly with urban households through organic A2 milk and fresh produce.

Designed the Organic Malleshwaram framework — offline activations, product sampling and PR campaigns around sustainability and healthy living — showing how agriculture, urban wellness and community identity combine through strategic communication.

06 Real Estate Property, protected digitally

E-DigiVault — a secure digital platform transforming property documentation from paperwork to protection. It stores district-level data across 30 districts, catalogues 4,000+ government services, and carries a defined step-by-step process for each — an operational playbook, not a passive record store. Built on ERPNext v15 across 13 role-based applications.

Chilume Legal & Liaisoning Services — established to help clients manage property-related legal paperwork and government liaisoning; positioned through PR as a trustworthy partner for legitimizing ownership, avoiding litigation and protecting assets.

07 Government Public systems, made public-friendly

Karnataka Examinations Authority — designed and produced the tutorial video series for KCET Counselling 2025, simplifying every step from document verification to seat allotment. 50,000+ students guided.

ISRO — curated and executed a high-profile interview with Dr. S. Somanath, then Chairman of ISRO, amplifying India’s space achievements for a youth audience.

Estify — software that solves civil-works estimate preparation against official Schedules of Rates: a searchable library of 3,200+ items across CPWD DSR-2023, Railways USSOR-2021 and four KPWD books, a guided four-step wizard, automated costing, and the full report set — BOQ, measurements, sub-head breakups and resource summaries, exportable to Excel.

08 Fashion AI meets the atelier

Maestro — an AI-powered fashion design platform taking teams from first spark to production-ready tech pack: Variant Engine, Text-to-Design, Sketch-to-Render, Prints & Patterns, on-body previews, a collaborative canvas, AI tech packs and Design Memory. Currently in early access.

World Fashion Exchange — developed the AI capabilities for WFX, the cloud fashion-technology suite (PLM, ERP, MES, traceability) serving 55,000+ users across 50+ countries.

Also built: THE SOLIST (luxury e-commerce for fine jewelry & watches with a Fifth Avenue showroom), Utthara (handloom sarees e-commerce), and ALTOMODA (curated luxury fashion platform).

09 Hospitality Stays that tell stories

Refinery Hotel, New York — designed and built the website for this luxury Manhattan property in the Garment District: 197 loft-style rooms, Parker & Quinn, the Refinery Rooftop, Winnie’s Jazz Club and the Hatbox — industrial heritage paired with a working hotel’s booking, offers and events functionality.

Global Malls — Divinity — end-to-end marketing across creative, digital and activation: brand creatives, the campaign website, and the launch event, delivered as one campaign.

10 E-commerce Commerce at scale

Shopperr — built the full technology stack for this India-based shopping and dropshipping platform spanning electronics, apparel, computers, books, watches and home & kitchen, with Amazon India integrated as a sales channel. Supports roughly 1,000 orders a day and ₹60 crore+ in revenue.

Naya Source — built the full stack for this B2B commerce platform: storefront, order management and backend infrastructure to run commerce at scale, integrated with Amazon India.
